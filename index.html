About

Hongchun Zeng is a physician-scientist in ultrasound medicine and diagnostic imaging. Her work brings together clinical ultrasonography, imaging research, and the training of physicians and graduate students.

She has served as Director of the Department of Ultrasound Medicine at Songjiang Hospital Affiliated to Shanghai Jiao Tong University School of Medicine. She holds a professorial title, is a Chief Physician in ultrasound medicine, and has served as a master’s graduate supervisor.

Her research centers on the clinical use of ultrasonography, particularly contrast-enhanced ultrasound for characterizing hepatic and renal lesions. She has studied hepatic alveolar echinococcosis, complex renal cystic lesions, and diagnostic imaging methods, and has published with collaborators in Austria and France.

Dr. Zeng has authored or co-authored more than 30 academic papers, served as chief editor of a medical book, and led research projects funded by the National Natural Science Foundation of China and at the provincial level. Her wider interests include evidence-based diagnostic imaging, interdisciplinary medical research, and the education of future healthcare professionals.

Research

Dr. Zeng’s research concerns the clinical applications of ultrasonography, with particular attention to contrast-enhanced techniques and to the characterization of hepatic and renal lesions.

  • Contrast-enhanced ultrasonography

    Clinical applications of contrast-enhanced ultrasound (CEUS) in tissue perfusion assessment, lesion characterization, and diagnostic decision-making.

  • Abdominal and hepatic imaging

    Ultrasound evaluation of liver disease, including hepatic alveolar echinococcosis and other focal hepatic lesions.

  • Renal imaging

    Characterization of complex renal cystic lesions, particularly with contrast-enhanced ultrasound and the Bosniak classification.

  • Thyroid and musculoskeletal ultrasound

    Clinical ultrasound assessment of thyroid nodules and selected musculoskeletal conditions.

  • Medical education

    Training in diagnostic ultrasonography and clinical imaging interpretation, including problem-based learning.

Research funding

  • Principal investigator of two provincial-level research projects
  • Participant in two further NSFC-funded projects

International collaboration

Her collaborative work includes a cardiac MRI study with pediatric radiologists and cardiologists at the Medical University of Graz, Austria (European Radiology, 2015), and a review of imaging in hepatic alveolar echinococcosis written with imaging and parasitology researchers in Besançon, France (Parasite, 2014). She also contributed to an interdisciplinary optogenetic-fMRI study published in Neuroscience Bulletin in 2026.

Teaching and mentoring

Dr. Zeng holds a professorial title and has served as a master’s graduate supervisor in China.

Her teaching covers diagnostic ultrasonography and clinical imaging interpretation for medical students and physicians. She has also studied how ultrasound skills are taught, including a 2019 paper on problem-based learning in ultrasound training for general practice.

She received an Excellent Teacher Award in 2022.

Academic service

Her professional service has included committee work, regional quality control, expert assessment, and journal peer review.

  • Member of Shanghai professional committees in abdominal, gastrointestinal, and translational ultrasound medicine
  • Leader of the Songjiang District Ultrasound Quality Control Group
  • Member of a Shanghai expert panel for medical malpractice assessment
  • Peer reviewer for Journal of Gannan Medical University and Journal of Xinjiang Medical University
